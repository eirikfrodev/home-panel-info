Privacy policy

Home panel is a private app used by a single household. Only that household's own Google accounts sign in to it.

What it accesses

With the household's permission, it reads the household's Google Calendar events and, for calendars the household owns, creates and updates events.

How the data is handled

  • Calendar data is stored only on the household's own computer at home.
  • It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Google user data is used only to show and manage the household's own calendars, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time under Google Account › Security › Third-party apps with account access. Deleting the app's local data on the household's computer removes everything it has stored.
